Who We Are

Mission

Amigos del Parque raises funds to maintain, enhance, and beautify the privately-funded Parque Biblioteca Boquete.

Vision

The Park welcomes all visitors to enjoy nature-inspired recreation, environmental education, and quiet reflection.

Values

Inclusive: We will keep the Park gates open to all who safeguard its beauty.

Philanthropic: We see the Park as a gift to Boquete and we will continue to attract generous donors to keep it evolving.

Inspirational: We inspire visitors to protect the natural environment that makes Chiriquí so exceptional.

Educational: We provide opportunities to learn about nature, ecosystems, and environmental stewardship.

The Focus of Our Efforts

Ways We Help

Fundraising

Amigos del Parque is dedicated to raising funds to both maintain the park and to help it develop and grow.

Education

Amigos del Parque Boquete recognizes the educational benefits of the park and will strive to keep this as a top priority.

Volunteers

Amigos del Parque Boquete organizes groups of volunteers to help when needed to ensure the park thrives.
