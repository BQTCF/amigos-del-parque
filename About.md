About Amigos del Parque
Boquete has a remarkable public park—free, open to everyone, and unlike any other in Panama. Parque Biblioteca Boquete covers 10 hectares (25 acres) along the Río Caldera, in downtown Boquete.
The developed side of the park invites you to slow down and enjoy nature. Wander along walking and jogging paths, relax beside ponds filled with turtles and fish, watch ducks and wild birds, or settle onto a shaded bench with a good book. There are picnic tables for families and friends, a meditation garden for reflection, and beautiful pathways that continue to grow and evolve. At the southern end, there are more streams and paths where green herons, acrobatic flycatchers, and colorful tanagers ply the streams and raise their families.
Across the river lies something just as special: protected forest. This untouched area provides a refuge for native plants and wildlife and helps sustain the park's rich biodiversity. Someday soon, we hope to enable visitors to explore this side of the park on peaceful woodland trails.
Parque Biblioteca Boquete exists because of the generosity of people who believe that every community deserves beautiful public spaces. The land was donated or purchased with private funds, and every path, bridge, garden, and tree has been made possible through private support.
The park is owned and operated by the Fundación Biblioteca de Boquete, a nonprofit organization that receives no annual funding from municipal, provincial, or national governments. Every dollar needed to maintain and improve the park comes from individuals, families, businesses, and foundations who care about preserving this extraordinary place.
Keeping the park beautiful is an ongoing commitment. Lawns must be mowed, gardens tended, trees planted, bridges maintained, and trails improved. At the same time, we continue working toward the park's long-term vision: new woodland trails, additional gathering spaces, environmental education, and other projects that will serve future generations.
That is why Amigos del Parque exists. As the park's volunteer fundraising organization, we help ensure that Parque Biblioteca Boquete remains free, beautiful, and growing for everyone.
We invite you to become part of that story. Your support helps protect this special place today—and build its future.
