Who We Are

About Us

Amigos del Parque is led by a volunteer Board of Directors united by a shared commitment to preserving, improving, and expanding Parque Biblioteca Boquete for the benefit of our community. We bring a wide range of backgrounds and experience, but we all share one goal: helping ensure that this remarkable park continues to thrive for generations to come.

Meet the Board

Board of Directors

Penelope Barrett, Treasurer

Penelope Barrett, Treasurer, has been a Boquete resident for more than two decades and has dedicated countless hours to the community through her involvement with many of Boquete's nonprofit organizations. She currently serves as Treasurer of the Boquete Handicap Foundation and is an active member of the Amigos del Parque committee, helping advance the mission of Parque Biblioteca Boquete.

Paquita Bath

Paquita Bath moved to Boquete in 2016 for its natural beauty and great community. She pursues her passion for conservation through her company Aligning Visions. She lives in Volcancito with her husband, Martin Moreno, and their 3 "Panamutts."

Nicolás Choy

Nicolás Choy is the general manager of Jarquin, S.A. in David. He serves as President of the Fundación Biblioteca de Boquete, is an active member of Amigos del Parque, and an enthusiastic reader.

Ajit Mathew George

Ajit Mathew George is a philanthropist, retired entrepreneur, and food and wine enthusiast who has founded and produced numerous successful community, culinary, and fundraising events, including Meals From the Masters Celebrity Chef's Brunch, the MidAtlantic Wine + Food Festival, and TEDxWilmington. He divides his time between Wilmington, Delaware, the British Virgin Islands, and Boquete, Panama.

Richard Lipner

Richard Lipner is co-owner of Finca Dos Jefes and has called the El Salto area of Boquete home since 2002. A firm believer in social justice and fair labor practices, he has built those values into his coffee business and beyond. He founded an educational initiative in the Ngäbe-Buglé Comarca that provides support for the educational and nutritional needs of local families.

Carlos Alberto and Ana Teresa Mendez

Carlos Alberto and Ana Teresa Mendez are retired bankers who have been deeply involved in a number of Panamanian nonprofits. They live in Panamá, Boquete, and spend part of the year in Florida with their daughter and grandchildren.

Tony Royle

Tony Royle, of British origin, is the owner of TR Services and has been living in Boquete for 19 years. Tony has been a driving force behind Parque Biblioteca Boquete since its earliest days. Instrumental in transforming the park from an idea on paper into the remarkable refuge it is today, he oversees day-to-day operations while continuing to envision, design, and manage new infrastructure projects that enhance the park's beauty and visitor experience.

Karen Rubinstein, President, and Barbara Rubinstein, Secretary

Karen Rubinstein, President, and Barbara Rubinstein, Secretary, are retired New Yorkers who moved to Boquete in 2019, drawn by its natural beauty and exceptional birdlife. They watched Parque Biblioteca Boquete evolve from a dry, rocky landscape into the remarkable community treasure it is today and quickly became passionate supporters of the park and its mission.

Linda Sherman

Linda Sherman is a retired Radio and Communications professional, living in Boquete since 2012. She seeks out Boquete's sacred places in order to preserve them.

Board members serve as volunteers and receive no compensation for their service.
