Why It Matters

Why Support the Parque Biblioteca Boquete?

The Parque Biblioteca Boquete was developed with private donations to provide a peaceful and inspiring natural environment in the center of Boquete for all of its residents and visitors. Private donations built the Park and are needed for its ongoing upkeep and growth. The Park is, and will continue to be, an enormous asset to the Boquete community with your support.

Amigos del Parque has been entrusted by the Biblioteca Foundation to raise the donations needed to maintain, enhance, and beautify the privately-funded Park for all. The future of the Park rests in your hands. Your generosity matters. There are so many ways you can support the Park with a charitable donation — and all donations are gratefully accepted!

Getting Started

Wondering How to Donate?

Anyone can make a donation by visiting our Donate page. There you can give online by credit card, debit card, or PayPal, or by bank transfer. You can even make your donation a recurring one — monthly, quarterly, or annually.

If you'd like to make a transfer from your Panamanian bank account, please send it to the Fundación's savings account (cuenta de ahorros) at Banco General, reserved for the Parque Biblioteca: 04-48-98-608521-8. If you do, please email us letting us know, along with your name and mobile number.

Good to Know

Tax Deductibility

For U.S. Tax Payers

Amigos del Parque is the flagship project of the Boquete Community Fund, recognized as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization, EIN 93-4835414. To claim a gift as a deduction on your U.S. taxes, please keep your emailed gift receipt as your official record.

Federal EIN: 93-4835414  |  IRS Determination Letter

For Panamanian Tax Payers

Donations to the Fundación Biblioteca de Boquete are tax-deductible, based on Resolution No. 201-4196 of April 4, 2012. To get a tax deduction receipt, please provide the Fundación Biblioteca de Boquete your name and cédula number.

Transparency

How Will My Money Be Used?

The goal of Amigos del Parque is to cover the needed costs of all maintenance and to bring in additional donations for new plantings and improved infrastructure. Contributions are allocated to Park maintenance, covering expenses such as gardeners and grounds staff, plantings, irrigation, cleaning, and signage. A portion of total donations is allocated to an endowment, whose returns will cover the Park's recurring maintenance costs over the long term.

Beyond a Standard Gift

Additional Ways to Give

Any amount

Endow the Park

We're building an endowment to cover the recurring costs of Park maintenance over the long term. When large enough, interest earned will be allocated annually to pay for Park expenses — offering greater economic stability for the Fundación to take on new large-scale projects. A minimum of $1,000 will greatly help the endowment grow quickly.

$500 minimum

Adopt a Tree

A native tree will be planted on your behalf. Reforesting the Park with native trees enhances habitat for Boquete's birds and animals while purifying air, storing CO₂, and enhancing the beauty of the natural world. Your name will be engraved on a plaque on the giving wall.

$50 minimum

Make a Donation as a Gift

Provide a meaningful gift to a friend who would value being part of keeping the Park beautiful. A card with a photo of the Park, indicating a donation made in their name, will be provided.

$3,000 minimum / year

Business & Organizational Sponsorships

Businesses and organizations can donate money to support Park maintenance and growth. Annual contributions of $3,000 or more receive signage at the Park entrance and online advertising on our website.

$1,000–$1,500

Sponsor a Bench

Dedicate a bench in tribute to a loved one, to express appreciation, or to mark a milestone. Your words will be engraved on a slate plaque for a sponsorship period of 10 years. A specific location can be requested for $1,500.

Planned giving

Leave a Legacy

Planned giving bequests support significant infrastructure projects such as new gardens, pavilions, and bridges. Please contact your lawyer or boquetecommunityfund@gmail.com for more information about legacy giving opportunities.
