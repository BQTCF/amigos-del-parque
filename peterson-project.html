The Peterson Project

Honoring a legacy, expanding the park's future

Our Legacy

Honoring Susan and Price Peterson

The Peterson Project was established in 2026 to honor Susan and Price Peterson on their 85th and 90th birthdays and to celebrate the extraordinary legacy they have created for the people of Boquete. Long before Parque Biblioteca Boquete existed, the Petersons founded the Fundación Biblioteca de Boquete and helped create the community library that has become a center for learning, culture, and community life. They later turned their vision to the creation of the park, transforming what was once a rocky, undeveloped piece of land into one of Boquete's most treasured public spaces. Through their vision, generosity, and unwavering commitment to the community, they have enriched the lives of thousands of residents and visitors alike.

Looking Ahead

Expanding the Park

The Peterson Project continues that vision. Contributions to the project will help fund the construction of a crossing over the Caldera River and the development of trails through the beautiful forest beyond, opening an entirely new section of the park for visitors to explore. This expansion fulfills an important part of the Petersons' original dream, allowing future generations to experience even more of the park's remarkable natural beauty while honoring the lasting impact of two people whose generosity has helped shape the Boquete we know today.
